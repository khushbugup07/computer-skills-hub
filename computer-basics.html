🖥️ What is a Computer?

Let's understand the basic idea of a computer.

What is a Computer?

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it, stores it, and produces useful information.

How Does a Computer Work?

A computer generally follows four basic steps:

  1. Input: Data is entered into the computer.
  2. Processing: The computer works on the data.
  3. Storage: Information can be saved for later use.
  4. Output: The computer gives us useful results.

Where Are Computers Used?

Computers are used in schools, offices, hospitals, banks, shops, communication, entertainment and many other areas.
