🌐 Internet Basics

Learn how the internet helps us find information, communicate and explore websites.

What is the Internet?

The internet is a worldwide network that connects computers and devices so people can share information and communicate with each other.

Important Internet Terms

  • 🌐 Internet: A network connecting computers and devices around the world.
  • 🔎 Search Engine: A tool used to find information on the internet.
  • 🌍 Website: A collection of web pages available on the internet.
  • 🧭 Web Browser: A program used to open and explore websites.

🛡️ Safe Internet Use

We should use the internet carefully and protect our personal information.

  • Do not share passwords with strangers.
  • Be careful when clicking unknown links.
  • Do not share personal information on unknown websites.
  • Ask a trusted adult if something online feels unsafe.
