🖨️ Output Devices

Learn how a computer gives information back to us.

What is an Output Device?

An output device is a hardware device that presents information or results from a computer to the user.

Common Output Devices

  • 🖥️ Monitor: Displays text, images and videos.
  • 🔊 Speakers: Produce sound from the computer.
  • 🖨️ Printer: Produces a physical copy of information.
